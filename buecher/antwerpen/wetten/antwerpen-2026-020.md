## Context
From the City's press release that it updated its Climate Plan 2030. The release sets four targets for 2030 compared with 2021: 100 hectares more unpaved ground, 100 hectares more park, 100 hectares more tree crown cover and 100 new cool places. Said: 2026-10-07; by: Stad Antwerpen (Alderman for the environment Karim Bachar).

## Reading
“We willen” (we want) is a reservation: `voraussichtlich`, 0.80. “Tegen 2030” (by 2030) is read in favour of the City as by 31 December 2030; the base year 2021 comes from the neighbouring sentence of the same release. Yes if a City report (for example a monitoring report of the Climate Plan) shows at least 100 hectares of extra park since 2021 by then; otherwise No.
