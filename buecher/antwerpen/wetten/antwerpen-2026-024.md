## Context
From the City's press release that AG Vespa, with Flemish support, starts restoring the outside of the former town hall on the Moorkensplein (built 1886–1889, a protected monument since 1974). The City invests more than 3 million euro, Flanders adds a heritage grant of 300,000 euro. Works were to start on 26 October 2026 “if all goes to plan” and to “last until the end of 2027”. Said: 2026-10-23; by: Stad Antwerpen (AG Vespa).
