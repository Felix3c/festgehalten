The City of Helsinki keeps saying what will happen and when: a tramway, parks, a health
centre, a skate park, grants and decisions. This book writes it down, waits, and checks. It
only quotes what the City itself published in its own English news (hel.fi/en/news). A
statement goes in if it has a date or a number; intentions without either stay out. Each bet
asks only whether the date or number in the quote was met, never whether anyone meant it.

Why Helsinki: the keeper's books now cover very large cities and capitals only. Helsinki
comes after London, Paris, Hamburg, Munich, Vienna, Rome, Dublin and Copenhagen.

Sources: the English news list at https://www.hel.fi/en/news. On 18 October 2026 the keeper
read the 283 English news items from 19 April to 16 October 2026; 456 sentences named a future
date, twenty were checked word for word, seventeen became bets. Left out on purpose: strategy
periods, population forecasts, climate targets, exhibitions and events, promises made mainly
by others (the national museum, the ice hockey world championship), one promise the City later
corrected (Pihlajasaari beach), one competition step that depends on a later City Board
decision (Hanasaari), and three statements that are hard to check from outside (a programme
start, the end date of one construction site, and summer streets that are to continue “at
least” in 2027).

Language: this edition is in English, and so are the quotations, exactly as published by the
City in English. The question under each bet is the keeper's own wording and is the only thing
that is resolved.

Speakers in this book are the City of Helsinki as an institution, its committees and its
administrations. Names of officials are not used. A bet is resolved "No" only with a primary
source (news of the City, a decision in the City's decision archive, a published list,
timetable or opening hours) and a date; if in doubt, it is marked `strittig` (disputed).

Quotations remain (c) City of Helsinki (short quotation for review and discussion,
tekijänoikeuslaki § 22; the re-use terms of hel.fi were not checked by a lawyer); everything
else CC0. The source pages are not copied into this repository. For each source there is a
link to a Wayback Machine copy, and the SHA-256 checksum of the keeper's local copy. Quotes
were checked against the text after removing inline markup and collapsing repeated spaces.

The "Computer" in this book is a language model (Claude, Anthropic) with access to public
sources, not special software. It stands in the same list as the City, under the same rules.

Corrections: write to hallo@belegbar.eu (English and German are fine). Complaints are checked
within 48 hours and noted on the bet concerned; entries are never changed, only annotated.

The keeper takes no money from the institution measured here, with one exception: a
published fixed price for entries an institution lodges itself (festgehalten format v1, §8.4). So far:
none.

Expiry: an open bet expires two years after its check date (format §1.1, §2.3). Expired bets
do not count towards the hit rate, but they stay in the record.
