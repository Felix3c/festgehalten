## Context
The library closes on 16 November 2026 and must leave its current premises by the end of the year because of the renovation of Pukinmäenkaari Comprehensive School. The release adds: “The tenancy agreement for the temporary premises will come into effect at the start of 2027.” The premises are then renovated and furnished, and the exact opening date “will be announced later on”. In June the City aimed for March–April 2027; that promise stands as helsinki-2026-011. This bet is the earlier date. Said: 2026-10-29; by: City of Helsinki.

## Reading
“Scheduled”, and further down “The aim is for the library to be able to open its doors in the temporary premises in February”, is a reservation: `voraussichtlich`, 0.80. Yes if the library is open to the public in the new premises on any day up to 28 February 2027 (library website, City news). Limited opening hours and services count, as the release expects them. The youth centre is not part of this bet. `pruefung_am` is the day after the deadline.
