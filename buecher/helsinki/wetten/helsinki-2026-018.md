## Context
The Malmi ice hall is currently not used as an ice hall. The City is turning it into a temporary complex of about 3,700 square metres with an indoor skatepark of about 1,370 square metres and a recreation hall of the same size; it is to run until about spring 2028. So far Kontula has Helsinki's only indoor skatepark. A website with opening hours is to open on nuorten.hel.fi towards the end of 2026. Said: 2026-10-21 (updated 2026-10-22); by: City of Helsinki.
