## Context
Rotterdam lets all residents of AOW age travel free on public transport (RET and EBS). The City says the scheme has become too expensive and stops it; a new scheme for about 20,000 older residents with an income up to 110% of the social minimum is to follow (see rotterdam-2026-008). For the gap between the two dates the City, RET and EBS were still looking for a temporary solution. Said: 2026-10-16; by: Gemeente Rotterdam.

## Reading
A plain statement with a fixed day: `angekuendigt`, 1.00. Yes if free travel for all AOW residents no longer applies from 1 January 2027. If the scheme for all is extended, or a temporary solution keeps free travel open for all AOW residents, No; a temporary solution for a smaller group does not change Yes. `pruefung_am` is the day after the deadline.
