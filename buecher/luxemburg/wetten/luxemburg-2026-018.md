## Context
From the City's news item that a section of the national cycle path “PC 1” is closed, between Bridel (car park in the rue de Kopstal) and the Juegdschlass site, for renovation and concrete works by the Administration des ponts et chaussées (the national roads administration). The cycle path and the forest paths concerned are to be open again from 30 July 2027. The works are the State's; the City is the speaker because it gives the date on its own website. Said: 2026-10-27; by: Ville de Luxembourg.

## Reading
A plain sentence, but with “selon les prévisions actuelles” (according to current forecasts): `voraussichtlich`, 0.80. “À partir du 30 juillet 2027” (from 30 July 2027, a Friday) is read as open by the next day, Saturday 31 July 2027. Yes if a City or State source (vdl.lu, secuforet.vdl.lu, Administration des ponts et chaussées) or a dated photo shows the section open to cyclists by then; a new closure date or no sign of reopening is No.
