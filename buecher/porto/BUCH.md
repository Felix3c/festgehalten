Porto keeps saying what it will do and when: a new parking policy, more bus lanes, new
directors for its theatre and gallery, a health hub in Campanhã, the headquarters of a
round-the-world sailing race. This book writes it down, waits, and checks. It quotes one
speaker: the City of Porto (Câmara Municipal do Porto), on its own news portal. A statement goes
in if it has a date; intentions without one stay out. Each bet asks only whether the date in the
quote was met, never whether anyone meant it.

Why Porto: the keeper's books now cover very large cities and capitals only. Porto, the second
city of Portugal, comes after London, Paris, Hamburg, Munich, Vienna, Rome, Dublin, Copenhagen,
Helsinki, Oslo, Brussels and Lyon.

Sources: the sitemap of www.porto.pt listed 354 news items of the “Portal de Notícias do Porto”
(last changed 22 August to 19 October 2026); all 354 were read on 20 and 21 October 2026. In them
the keeper looked for sentences that name a future date together with a promise word (110
candidates), then read each candidate in its item. Eleven became bets. The date a statement was
said is the publication date the page gives (`article:published_time`); the portal may update
this date when a page is republished, so it is the latest publication, not necessarily the first.
On 28 October 2026 the keeper read the 49 items the sitemap listed as changed from 20 to 27
October (18 candidate sentences); one became a bet (porto-2026-012), one added a note to
porto-2026-007.

Two things the City says twice, differently. On 3 September 2026 it counted 5.8 km of new bus
lanes for 2026; on 16 September it promised to grow the bus lanes from 15 to 21 km by the end of
the year. Both stand as separate bets (porto-2026-006 and -007). On 29 September 2026 the City
wrote that The Ocean Race will start from Porto in 2031, 2035 and 2039; on 7 October it wrote
2030, 2034 and 2038, plus a Special Race in 2028. Both pages were still online in this form on
22 October 2026. The start years are not bets (the race is run by its organisers); the move of
its headquarters is (porto-2026-010).

Left out on purpose: works and plans of other bodies that the City only reports (the Coliseu
renovation, 200 officers of the national police PSP, a survey of the bus company STCP, the
handball and football championships, The Ocean Race in Alicante); deadlines of calls, tenders,
applications and public consultations; events, exhibitions and courses; goals without a project
(climate neutrality in 2030) or with only a budget (five health centres “até 2031”); sentences
with “poderá” (may). Also left out because they are hard to check from outside: a survey of trees
at schools “até ao final de outubro” and the hand-over of the Matadouro cultural centre “no
final de 2026” (its news item says “2027” in the title and “2017” in the text, a typing error).

Reading the dates: a fixed day counts to that day; a month to its last day; “até ao final do
ano” and “até dezembro” (by the end of the year, by December) to 31 December; “até 2029” to
31 December 2029; “a partir do próximo ano” (from next year), said in 2026, to 31 December 2027.
A quoted sentence with “previsto/prevista” (planned) or “prevendo” (expecting) counts as said
with a reservation (`voraussichtlich`, 0.80); a plain statement counts as announced
(`angekuendigt`, 1.00). Only the quoted sentence decides.

Language: this edition is in English. Quotations stay in the original Portuguese, exactly as
published. The question under each bet is the keeper's own wording and is the only thing that is
resolved. English glosses of Portuguese words in the text are the keeper's.

The speaker in this book is the Câmara Municipal do Porto as an institution. Names of officials
are used only to name a source. A bet is resolved "No" only with a primary source (a page or news
item of the City or of a municipal company, a decision of the City Council, a dated photo) and a
date; if in doubt, it is marked `strittig` (disputed). Pages of the City are updated in place, so
an entry can also be resolved by a dated copy of the page.

Quotations: Source: www.porto.pt, Portal de Notícias do Porto. The legal notice of the portal
(“Avisos legais”) allows quoting parts of its texts and using the information of its news,
provided the source is named clearly and permanently in this form; every bet carries this line
(the keeper's reading, not checked by a lawyer). Everything else CC0. The source pages are not
copied into this repository. For each source there is a link to a Wayback Machine copy, and the
SHA-256 checksum of the keeper's local copy. Quotes were checked against the text after removing
inline markup and collapsing repeated spaces.

The "Computer" in this book is a language model (Claude, Anthropic) with access to public
sources, not special software. It stands in the same list as the City, under the same rules.

Corrections: write to hallo@belegbar.eu (Portuguese, English and German are fine). Complaints are
checked within 48 hours and noted on the bet concerned; entries are never changed, only
annotated.

The keeper takes no money from the institutions measured here, with one exception: a published
fixed price for entries an institution lodges itself (festgehalten format v1, §8.4). So far:
none.

Expiry: an open bet expires two years after its check date (format §1.1, §2.3). Expired bets do
not count towards the hit rate, but they stay in the record.
