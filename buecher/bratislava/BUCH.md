Bratislava keeps saying what it will do and when: 20 new gas buses by the end of October and 15 more
by December, a skatepark under the Apollo bridge finished in autumn, works on the Ružinov tram line
from the start of 2027, the Grössling baths rebuilt by the end of 2028. This book writes it down,
waits, and checks. It quotes one speaker: the Capital City of the Slovak Republic Bratislava (Hlavné
mesto Slovenskej republiky Bratislava), in the news items on its own website, including what it
says about its own companies and institute: the transport company DPB, the waste company OLO and the
Metropolitan Institute MIB. A statement goes in if it has a date; intentions without one stay out.
Each bet asks only whether the date in the quote was met, never whether anyone meant it.

Why Bratislava: the keeper's books now cover very large cities and capitals only. Bratislava, the
capital of Slovakia, comes after books on other European capitals and large cities, most recently
Zagreb, Ljubljana and Naples.

Sources: the news items of bratislava.sk (“Aktuality”, at bratislava.sk/spravy/, in Slovak). The
website's robots.txt (read 19 October 2026) excludes only /api/; this book used nothing there. The
news list loads by JavaScript, so the keeper took the list of items from the website's sitemap
(newest entry dated 13 October 2026, so later items may be missing) and read the 131 items dated 20
April to 19 October 2026, on 19 October 2026. Many items are articles of the City's magazine in.ba
and appear in batches on a few days. In them the keeper looked for sentences that name a future date
together with a promise word (110 sentences in 60 items), then read each candidate in its item.
Thirteen sentences were checked live and in the archive; twelve became bets. The items carry the
author line “Magistrát” (City Hall); the sender is the City. The date a statement was said is the
publication date on the item's own page. One item, on the fleet of DPB, gives three bets
(bratislava-2026-001, -004 and -008).

Left out on purpose: works of other bodies, even when the City reports them (a foundation of the
water company BVS); the renewal of the main building at Mičurin, because the City Council decided on
18 June 2026 to let the building, so the works lie with the tenant; a new by-law on pawnshops from 1
November (a legal act, not a promise); deadlines that had already passed on 19 October 2026 (two
park-and-ride sites “in mid-October”, trolleybus line 32 from 3 October); conditional plans of DPB;
exhibitions, festivals and other events with a closing day.

Reading the dates: “do konca októbra” (by the end of October) means by 31 October 2026; “v
priebehu novembra 2026” (during November 2026) by 30 November 2026; “na jeseň” (in autumn) is read
as meteorological autumn, so by 30 November; “do decembra”, “do konca roka 2026” (by December, by
the end of 2026) and a planting season “od konca októbra do decembra” (from the end of October to
December) as by 31 December 2026; “od začiatku roku 2027” (from the beginning of 2027) as by 31
March 2027; “na budúci rok”, “v budúcom roku” (next year) as by 31 December 2027; “do konca roka
2028” and “v r. 2028” (in 2028) as by 31 December 2028. Each bet says so. Plain sentences and plain
list entries (“bude”, “budú”, “prevezme”, will be, will take over) count as announced
(`angekuendigt`, 1.00). A sentence with “by mal”, “by malo” (should), “bude možné” (it will be
possible), “chce” (wants) or “cieľom zostáva” (the aim remains) counts as said with a reservation
(`voraussichtlich`, 0.80). Where one sentence or list holds several statements, the bet names the
part it rests on. Only the quoted sentence decides.

Language: this edition is in English. Quotations stay in the original Slovak, exactly as published,
including the dashes and list markers the City used. The question under each bet is the keeper's own
wording and is the only thing that is resolved. English glosses of Slovak words in the text are the
keeper's; they were made with the help of a language model and have not yet been checked by a
native speaker.

The speaker in this book is the City of Bratislava as an institution. Names of officials are used
only to name a source. A bet is resolved "No" only with a primary source (a news item or page of
the City, DPB, OLO or MIB, a decision of the City Council, a procurement notice, a permit, a dated
photo) and a date; if in doubt, it is marked `strittig` (disputed). Pages of the City may be updated
in place, so an entry can also be resolved by a dated copy of the page.

Quotations remain (c) Hlavné mesto Slovenskej republiky Bratislava; the pages say “© 2026 Hlavné
mesto Slovenskej republiky Bratislava”. The keeper found no terms of use and no licence for the
texts of bratislava.sk (footer, privacy and accessibility pages and the sitemap searched on 19
October 2026), so the terms are unclear. This book takes from each item one sentence or list entry,
with the title of the item, its date and a link, for criticism and review; that is what the
quotation exception in EU law allows (Directive 2001/29/EC, Article 5(3)(d)). The keeper could not
open the text of the Slovak Copyright Act (Act No. 185/2015 Coll.) on 19 October 2026 and has not
checked how Slovakia implements the exception (not checked by a lawyer). This book uses no pictures
or coats of arms. It is not endorsed by the City of Bratislava; the City has not agreed with it or
with any question in it. Everything else CC0. The source pages are not copied into this repository.
For each source there is a link to a Wayback Machine copy and the SHA-256 checksum of the keeper's
local copy. Quotes were checked against the text of the page after removing markup, decoding
entities and collapsing repeated spaces.

The "Computer" in this book is a language model (Claude, Anthropic) with access to public
sources, not special software. It stands in the same list as the City, under the same rules.

Corrections: write to hallo@belegbar.eu (Slovak, English and German are fine). Complaints are
checked within 48 hours and noted on the bet concerned; entries are never changed, only
annotated.

The keeper takes no money from the institutions measured here, with one exception: a published
fixed price for entries an institution lodges itself (festgehalten format v1, §8.4). So far:
none.

Expiry: an open bet expires two years after its check date (format §1.1, §2.3). Expired bets do
not count towards the hit rate, but they stay in the record.
