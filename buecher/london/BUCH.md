Transport for London and the Mayor of London keep saying what will happen and when:
openings, start dates, completion dates, decisions. This book writes it down, waits, and
checks. It only quotes what TfL and the Mayor said in their own press releases. A statement goes in if it has a date or a
number; intentions without either stay out. Each bet asks only whether the date or number
in the quote was met, never whether anyone meant it.

Why London: the keeper's books now cover very large cities and capitals only. London came
first because its transport projects are followed far beyond the city and because nobody
appeared to be keeping a running record of these dates (search of 5 October 2026).

Sources: the monthly press release lists of TfL at
https://tfl.gov.uk/info-for/media/press-releases/2026/january (and the following months).
On 7 October 2026 the lists for January to September 2026 showed 105 releases; the October
list was still empty. The keeper read the titles of all 105 and the full text of 26 that
looked as if they named a later date; twelve bets come from eleven of them. Many releases
name only dates in the next few days or none at all. Where the Mayor speaks in a TfL release, the release is the source.

The Mayor's own press releases were added on 8 October 2026, from the list at
https://www.london.gov.uk/media-centre/mayors-press-releases. On 7 October 2026 it showed
101 releases from 7 January to 7 October 2026. The keeper read the titles of all 101 and
the full text of 32; seven bets (london-2026-013 to -019) come from seven of them. One of them
is a release of the Old Oak and Park Royal Development Corporation, the Mayor's development
corporation, published on london.gov.uk; it is counted under the Mayor.

Speakers in this book are Transport for London as an institution or the Mayor of London in
office. Names of officials are not used. Bets are about dates and numbers in the quote, never
about intentions or honesty. A bet is resolved "No" only with a primary source (TfL release,
Board paper, traffic order) and a date; if in doubt, it is marked `strittig` (disputed).

Quotations remain (c) TfL / GLA (s. 30(1ZA) CDPA 1988); everything else CC0.
The source pages are not copied into this repository. For each source there is a link to a
Wayback Machine copy where one could be made, and the SHA-256 checksum of the keeper's local
copy, fetched on the day the bet was entered. Quotes were checked against the page text
after collapsing repeated spaces; where a quote joins two sentences of the same release,
"…" stands between them.

The "Computer" in this book is a language model (Claude, Anthropic) with access to public
sources, not special software. It stands in the same list as TfL, under the same rules.

Corrections: write to hallo@belegbar.eu (English is fine). Complaints are checked within
48 hours and noted on the bet concerned; entries are never changed, only annotated.

The keeper takes no money from the institution measured here, with one exception: a
published fixed price for entries an institution lodges itself (festgehalten format v1, §8.4). So far:
none.

Expiry: an open bet expires two years after its check date (format §1.1, §2.3). Expired bets
do not count towards the hit rate, but they stay in the record.
