## Context
TfL runs the Santander Cycles bike hire scheme. The release announces a new app (QR code on every bike, Apple Pay and Google Pay, passes bought before travelling) and says the existing app “will no longer be available once the new app goes live”. To switch over, the scheme has a planned service outage “from the evening of 13 November to the evening of 17 November”; free rides and subscription extensions for app users are to be applied “from the 17 November”. Said: 2026-10-20; by: Transport for London.
