## Context
TfL and Boldyn Networks (20-year concession from June 2021) are rolling out mobile coverage in Tube stations and tunnels. In June 2026 about 60 per cent of below-ground stations had coverage. The release also quotes TfL: "We remain focused on having 4G and 5G mobile coverage across the whole Tube network by the end of the year".
