## Context
On Monday 26 October 2026 Oxford Street became traffic-free between Orchard Street and Great Portland Street. The release calls this the first stage of a phased programme with a temporary “transitional scheme”, and minimal construction during the Christmas period. The Oxford Street Development Corporation (set up by the Mayor in January 2026) leads the regeneration; TfL maintains the street. Said: 2026-10-26; by: Mayor of London.
