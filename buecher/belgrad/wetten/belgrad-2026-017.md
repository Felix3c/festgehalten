## Context
From the City's item of 20 October 2026 on Mayor Aleksandar Šapić's visit to the works on the Novi srpski most (the new Sava bridge that replaces the old Savski most) and the extension of Ulica Vladimira Popovića. The Mayor said the pace of works was back to what it was before a stop after an incident some months earlier, that piles and pillars were being cast, and that the final deadline for the bridge, the connecting roads, Ulica Vladimira Popovića and Karađorđeva was spring 2027.
