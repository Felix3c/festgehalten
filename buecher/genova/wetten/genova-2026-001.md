## Context
From the City's item on the start of the works on the sea wall at the foot of the cliff of via Capolungo (Nervi), where a landslide happened in 2014. The rocks arrive by land at Multedo and are taken to Capolungo by pontoon; the City pays about 1 million euro. The councillor for public works, Massimo Ferrante, calls this sea protection a first step; the vertical part of the cliff is to be secured later. The City's later items up to 23 October 2026 (read 24 October) did not report the end of the works. Said: 2026-06-23; by: Comune di Genova.

## Reading
“È prevista” (is foreseen) is a reservation: `voraussichtlich`, 0.80. “Entro l’ottobre 2026” (by October 2026) is read as by 31 October 2026. The bet is only about the sea wall (the works named in the item), not about securing the vertical cliff. Yes if a City source (news item, decision, notice) or a dated photo shows the sea-wall works finished by then; a later City source that gives an end date on or before 31 October also counts.
