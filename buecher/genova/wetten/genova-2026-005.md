## Context
From the City's item on the City's executive taking note of the positive outcome of the decisive services conference on the Stadio Luigi Ferraris redevelopment, proposed by Genova Stadium S.r.l. (owned in equal shares by Genoa CFC and UC Sampdoria). The City Council was to decide in September on the land, the economic terms and the surface right; the executive approved its proposal on the surface right on 9 September 2026 (see genova-2026-013). The same sentence names the award by the end of March 2027 (genova-2026-013). Said: 2026-07-30; by: Comune di Genova.

## Reading
“Procederà” (will proceed) is a plain sentence: `angekuendigt`, 1.00. “Il prossimo autunno” (next autumn) is read as by 20 December 2026, the last day of autumn. The bet rests on the part “mettere a base di gara” (putting out to tender), not on the award. Yes if the call for tenders is published by then (procurement notice or City source with date).
