Zagreb keeps saying what it will do and when: a new tram line on Sarajevska cesta next month, 20
new kindergartens in this term by the end of 2026, a pedestrian Vlaška ulica by New Year, 288
affordable-rent flats in Podbrežje by the middle of 2027, the Paromlin library in the second half
of 2027. This book writes it down, waits, and checks. It quotes one speaker: the City of Zagreb
(Grad Zagreb, with Mayor Tomislav Tomašević and his deputy mayors), in the news items on its own
website. A statement goes in if it has a date; intentions without one stay out. Each bet asks only
whether the date in the quote was met, never whether anyone meant it.

Why Zagreb: the keeper's books now cover very large cities and capitals only. Zagreb, the capital
of Croatia, comes after books on other European capitals and large cities, most recently Edinburgh,
Sofia and Ljubljana.

Sources: the news items of www.zagreb.hr (“Gradske vijesti”, in Croatian). The website has no
robots.txt (the address returns “not found”, read 19 October 2026). The City publishes about one
and a third news items a day, so the keeper read all 234 items dated 20 April to 19 October 2026,
on 19 October 2026. In them the keeper looked for sentences that name a future date together with
a promise word (93 sentences in 65 items, plus a second pass by hand), then read each candidate in
its item. Nineteen sentences were checked live and in the archive; seventeen became bets. The news
items carry no author line; the sender is the City. The date a statement was said is the date on
the item's own page.

Left out on purpose: works of other bodies or shared with them, even when the City reports them
(the Maksimir stadium with the Government of Croatia, the public bike system, national roads and
registers, the university); events and calls with a closing day; deadlines that had already passed
on 19 October 2026; strategies and programmes without a checkable end (culture to 2030, a fully
electric bus fleet “in the next ten years”); several building starts “in autumn” or “by the end of
the year” that are hard to check from outside; a new sports hall at Stjepan Benceković primary
school promised for this autumn, because the keeper could not find out whether it was already in
use on 19 October 2026; and linking all 487 traffic-light junctions to the control system by 2028,
which cannot be checked from outside.

Reading the dates: “sljedeći mjesec” (next month), said in October, means by 30 November 2026;
“ovu jesen”, “na jesen” (this autumn, in autumn) are read as meteorological autumn, by 30
November; “do kraja (ove) godine” and “krajem godine” (by / at the end of the year) as 31 December
2026; “početkom 2027.” (at the beginning of 2027) in favour of the City as by 31 March 2027;
spring and summer as meteorological spring and summer (by 31 May and 31 August); “sredinom sljedeće
godine” (in the middle of next year) as by 30 June 2027; “sljedeće godine”, “tijekom 2027.”, “u
drugoj polovini 2027.” and “do kraja sljedeće godine” (next year, during 2027, in the second half
of 2027, by the end of next year) as by 31 December 2027. Each bet says so. Plain sentences (“će”,
“ćemo”, will, we will; “otvara se”, opens) count as announced (`angekuendigt`, 1.00). A quoted
sentence with “planirano”, “predviđeno”, “očekuje se”, “očekujemo” (planned, foreseen, is expected,
we expect) or “trebale bi” (should) counts as said with a reservation (`voraussichtlich`, 0.80).
Where one sentence holds two statements, the bet names the part it rests on. Only the quoted
sentence decides.

Language: this edition is in English. Quotations stay in the original Croatian, exactly as
published. The question under each bet is the keeper's own wording and is the only thing that is
resolved. English glosses of Croatian words in the text are the keeper's; they were made with the
help of a language model and have not yet been checked by a native speaker.

The speaker in this book is the City of Zagreb as an institution. Names of officials are used
only to name a source. A bet is resolved "No" only with a primary source (a news item or page of
the City, a decision of the City Assembly, a notice in the City's Official Gazette, a procurement
notice, a permit, a dated photo) and a date; if in doubt, it is marked `strittig` (disputed). Pages
of the City may be updated in place, so an entry can also be resolved by a dated copy of the page.

Quotations remain (c) Grad Zagreb. The City's terms of use (“Uvjeti korištenja”, www.zagreb.hr,
dated 29 December 2020, read 19 October 2026) allow original documents to be taken without
restriction, except the City's official marks and content taken from other sources, provided the
source is named with a link to www.zagreb.hr. This book does that for every quote and uses no
pictures or coats of arms. Quoting also rests on the Croatian Copyright and Related Rights Act
(Zakon o autorskom pravu i srodnim pravima, Narodne novine 111/2021, Article 202 “Citati, kritike i
osvrti”: verbatim excerpts of lawfully published works may be used for criticism, review and the
like, to the extent justified by the purpose, naming the source and the author) (version of 2021;
whether later amendments touch it was not checked; not checked by a lawyer). This book is not
endorsed by the City of Zagreb; the City has not agreed with it or with any question in it.
Everything else CC0. The source pages are not copied into this repository. For each source there is
a link to a Wayback Machine copy and the SHA-256 checksum of the keeper's local copy. Quotes were
checked against the text of the page after removing markup, decoding entities and collapsing
repeated spaces.

The "Computer" in this book is a language model (Claude, Anthropic) with access to public
sources, not special software. It stands in the same list as the City, under the same rules.

Corrections: write to hallo@belegbar.eu (Croatian, English and German are fine). Complaints are
checked within 48 hours and noted on the bet concerned; entries are never changed, only
annotated.

The keeper takes no money from the institutions measured here, with one exception: a published
fixed price for entries an institution lodges itself (festgehalten format v1, §8.4). So far:
none.

Expiry: an open bet expires two years after its check date (format §1.1, §2.3). Expired bets do
not count towards the hit rate, but they stay in the record.
