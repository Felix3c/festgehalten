## Context
The City began demolishing the 51-year-old prefabricated building of the kindergarten in Cerska ulica 22 in Dubrava. In its place a new kindergarten for 16 groups (one more than before) is to be built, about 3,000 m², works and equipment about 9.8 million euro with VAT, paid entirely from the City budget. The children were given places in other City kindergartens during the works. Said: 2026-10-23; by: Grad Zagreb.
