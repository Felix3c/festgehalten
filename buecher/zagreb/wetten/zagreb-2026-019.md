## Context
The City officially started drawing up its Sustainable Urban Mobility Plan with a kick-off meeting. The plan is drawn up under a contract of 288,000 euro by Ernst & Young Savjetovanje and the Faculty of Transport and Traffic Sciences of the University of Zagreb, with the civil-society organisation ODRAZ and the firm INTERDIS; workshops and focus groups with residents are planned. The result is to be a document with a five-year action plan. The same item notes that the EU's TEN-T regulation expects such plans for the network's urban nodes by the end of 2027. Said: 2026-10-22; by: Grad Zagreb.
