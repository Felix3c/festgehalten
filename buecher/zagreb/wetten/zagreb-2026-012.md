## Context
Said by Mayor Tomislav Tomašević at the building site in Podbrežje: the largest building for affordable rent under construction in Croatia, 288 flats, three business premises, 328 parking spaces, 42 million euro without VAT, partly funded by a loan from the European Investment Bank. The same item says all works are expected to be finished by the middle of next year. Said: 2026-10-13; by: Grad Zagreb (Mayor Tomislav Tomašević).
