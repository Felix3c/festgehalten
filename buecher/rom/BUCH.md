The City of Rome (Roma Capitale) keeps saying what will happen and when: openings, works,
new services, numbers of trees and sensors. This book writes it down, waits, and checks. It
only quotes what Roma Capitale itself published in its own news (Notizie on comune.roma.it,
edited by the City's registered news agency). A statement goes in if it has a date or a
number; intentions without either stay out. Each bet asks only whether the date or number in
the quote was met, never whether anyone meant it.

Why Rome: the keeper's books now cover very large cities and capitals only. Rome comes after
London, Paris, Hamburg, Munich and Vienna.

Sources: the news list at https://www.comune.roma.it/web/it/notizie.page (ten per page). On
17 October 2026 the keeper read all 560 news items from 14 July to 16 October 2026; 100
sentences named a future date, twelve were checked word for word, nine became bets. Left out
on purpose: events, exhibitions and calls for applications, budget figures, targets for 2030
and later, and promises made by others (the State, Sport e Salute, the AS Roma football club,
the railway company RFI) even when the City reported them.

Language: this edition is in English. Quotations stay in the original Italian, exactly as
published, including the typographic apostrophe (’) where the City used it. The question
under each bet is the keeper's own wording and is the only thing that is resolved.

Speakers in this book are Roma Capitale as an institution or the Mayor of Rome in office.
Names of officials are not used. A bet is resolved "No" only with a primary source (news of
the City, deliberation of the Giunta or the Assemblea Capitolina, a published list) and a
date; if in doubt, it is marked `strittig` (disputed).

Quotations remain (c) Roma Capitale (short quotation for criticism and discussion, art. 70
Legge 633/1941); everything else CC0. The source pages are not copied into this repository.
For each source there is a link to a Wayback Machine copy, and the SHA-256 checksum of the
keeper's local copy. Quotes were checked against the text after removing inline markup and
collapsing repeated spaces.

The "Computer" in this book is a language model (Claude, Anthropic) with access to public
sources, not special software. It stands in the same list as the City, under the same rules.

Corrections: write to hallo@belegbar.eu (Italian and English are fine). Complaints are
checked within 48 hours and noted on the bet concerned; entries are never changed, only
annotated.

The keeper takes no money from the institution measured here, with one exception: a
published fixed price for entries an institution lodges itself (festgehalten format v1, §8.4). So far:
none.

Expiry: an open bet expires two years after its check date (format §1.1, §2.3). Expired bets
do not count towards the hit rate, but they stay in the record.
