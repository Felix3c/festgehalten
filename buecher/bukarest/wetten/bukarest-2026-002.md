## Context
Bucharest is renewing 212 km of district-heating pipes (“termia”) with EU funds (POIM and PDD, 1.76 billion lei in total). The item says 95 % of the current sites are done and another 63 km are in progress. Said: 2026-09-14; by: Primăria Municipiului București.
