## Context
From the City's item on giving the detailed design (progetto esecutivo) of the tram sections “A nord” (a loop of about 5.3 km from Villa Sofia past the stadium) and “B” (rebuilding the line from piazzale Einstein to Notarbartolo station and on to piazza Giachery) to the contractors SIS and CAF. Reported by the councillor for mobility, Maurizio Carta; the item also quotes Mayor Roberto Lagalla. The plan foresees about 150 days for the design and 180 more days for the approvals of other bodies. Said: 2026-09-22; by: Comune di Palermo.

## Reading
“Sono previsti” (are foreseen) is a reservation: `voraussichtlich`, 0.80. “Tra agosto e settembre 2027” (between August and September 2027) is read as by 30 September 2027. Yes if a City source or a dated photo shows building works on either section begun by then (a handover of works, “consegna lavori”, with work on site); preparatory surveys or moving of utilities alone are No.
