The City of Copenhagen (Københavns Kommune) keeps saying what will happen and when: new
city-life streets, a school swimming hall, opening hours, a library, a bridge, chargers for
electric cars. This book writes it down, waits, and checks. It only quotes what the City
itself published in its own news (Nyheder on kk.dk). A statement goes in if it has a date or
a number; intentions without either stay out. Each bet asks only whether the date or number
in the quote was met, never whether anyone meant it.

Why Copenhagen: the keeper's books now cover very large cities and capitals only. Copenhagen
comes after London, Paris, Hamburg, Munich, Vienna, Rome and Dublin.

Sources: the news list at https://www.kk.dk/nyheder. On 18 October 2026 the keeper read the
115 news items from 19 February to 9 October 2026 (the City publishes about one item every
two days); 97 sentences named a future date, thirteen were checked word for word, eleven
became bets. Left out on purpose: budget periods and strategies, climate targets for 2035,
projects due in 2029 or later, promises that cannot be checked from outside, one works
period that had already ended when this book was opened (Slesvigsgade, weeks 27-41), the
start of works on six further city-life streets (spring 2027; a start is hard to see from
outside) and the closure of Vesterbro Passage to cars “until December” (an urban trial that
may be extended or made permanent; how to read it is still open).

Language: this edition is in English. Quotations stay in the original Danish, exactly as
published. The question under each bet is the keeper's own wording and is the only thing
that is resolved. English glosses of Danish words in the text are the keeper's.

Speakers in this book are Københavns Kommune as an institution or its administrations.
Names of officials are not used. A bet is resolved "No" only with a primary source (news of
the City, a decision of the City Council or a committee, a published list or opening hours)
and a date; if in doubt, it is marked `strittig` (disputed).

Quotations remain (c) Københavns Kommune (short quotation for review and discussion,
ophavsretsloven § 22; kk.dk publishes no re-use terms; not checked by a lawyer); everything
else CC0. The source pages are not copied into this repository. For each source there is a
link to a Wayback Machine copy, and the SHA-256 checksum of the keeper's local copy. Quotes
were checked against the text after removing inline markup and collapsing repeated spaces.

The "Computer" in this book is a language model (Claude, Anthropic) with access to public
sources, not special software. It stands in the same list as the City, under the same rules.

Corrections: write to hallo@belegbar.eu (Danish, English and German are fine). Complaints are
checked within 48 hours and noted on the bet concerned; entries are never changed, only
annotated.

The keeper takes no money from the institution measured here, with one exception: a
published fixed price for entries an institution lodges itself (festgehalten format v1, §8.4). So far:
none.

Expiry: an open bet expires two years after its check date (format §1.1, §2.3). Expired bets
do not count towards the hit rate, but they stay in the record.
