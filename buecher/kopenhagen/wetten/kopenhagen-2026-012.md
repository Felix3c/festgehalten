## Context
KAI is a 5,800 m² water culture house with 10 pools on Papirøen in Copenhagen harbour, designed by Kengo Kuma & Associates. It costs DKK 989 million; the City contributes DKK 443 million and the Nordea Foundation DKK 546 million. The builder is the foundation Ejendomsfonden Vandkulturhuset Papirøen, in cooperation with the City. The release also says: “Information om program for indvielsen den 5. december 2026 vil blive offentliggjort på et senere tidspunkt.” and “Vandkulturhuset er fortsat under opførelse og er derfor lukket for offentligheden frem til åbningen.” Said: 2026-10-20; by: Københavns Kommune.

## Reading
“åbner den 5. december 2026” (opens on 5 December 2026) has no reservation: `angekuendigt`, 1.00. Yes if a source (City, kai.kk.dk, press) shows that members of the public could swim in KAI on or before 5 December 2026. An inauguration for invited guests only, without public admission on or before that day, is No. The outdoor bathing areas are not part of this bet (see kopenhagen-2026-013). `pruefung_am` is the day after the date.
