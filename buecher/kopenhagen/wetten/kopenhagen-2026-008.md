## Context
The land is to be developed into a new district (about 80,000 residents) with metro line M5 and an eastern ring road; the City hands its plots to By & Havn. The track is run by the municipal Fritidscenter Amager Strand. The City says it found no other site for a go-kart track. Said: 2026-05-12; by: Københavns Kommune.

## Reading
“kan anvendes frem til 1. juli 2028” (can be used until 1 July 2028) has no reservation: `angekuendigt`, 1.00. Yes if a source (City, By & Havn, the track or the leisure centre) shows the track closed from 1 July 2028. A later closure date, or the track still in use in July 2028, is No. `pruefung_am` is the day after the deadline.
