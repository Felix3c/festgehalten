## Context
Arena Skole og Fritid is a new school with sports hall, swimming hall and club rooms. The build was delayed; in December 2024 the City ended its contract with the builder BAM Danmark and handed the job to the partnership TRUST II. The release says the building is to be handed over by the end of September 2026 and the school moves in after the autumn holidays.
