## Context
The City started work on the plan with experts of the European Investment Bank; the same item says, as a heading, “Rīgas metropoles mobilitātes plāns jāizstrādā līdz 2027. gada beigām” (the plan must be drawn up by the end of 2027). The City leads the work, together with ministries, the Riga planning region, the surrounding municipalities and transport companies. Said: 2026-10-22; by: Rīgas valstspilsētas pašvaldība.

## Reading
“Plānots” (planned) is a reservation: `voraussichtlich`, 0.80. “Līdz 2027. gada beigām” is read as by 31 December 2027. Yes only if the plan is both finished and approved (adopted by Rīgas dome or by the body the City names as approving it) by that day, shown by a decision or a news item of the City; a draft, a public consultation or a finished but not yet approved plan is No. `pruefung_am` is the day after the deadline.
