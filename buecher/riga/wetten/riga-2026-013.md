## Context
The Vanšu bridge is Riga's cable-stayed bridge over the Daugava. During the works traffic is to use a temporary bridge, which the Mayor told the Public Space and Mobility Department to prepare. The same item gives the plan in three steps: river banks in the first half of 2027, then Krišjāņa Valdemāra iela, then the bridge itself in late 2027 (see riga-2026-020 for the end date). Said: 2026-09-21; by: Rīgas valstspilsētas pašvaldība.
