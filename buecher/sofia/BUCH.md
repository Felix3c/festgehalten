Sofia keeps saying what it will do and when: 8 new trains for metro line 3 by January 2027, a
plan for the city's mineral water by February 2027, the new metro station “Панчо Владигеров”
finished in summer 2027, more than 20 km of new cycle routes and no more shortage of kindergarten
places by the end of 2027. This book writes it down, waits, and checks. It quotes one speaker:
Sofia Municipality (Столична община, with Mayor Vasil Terziev, his deputy mayors, the districts
and the City's own companies such as Metropoliten), in the news items on its own website. A
statement goes in if it has a date; intentions without one stay out. Each bet asks only whether
the date in the quote was met, never whether anyone meant it.

Why Sofia: the keeper's books now cover very large cities and capitals only. Sofia, the capital
of Bulgaria, comes after books on other European capitals and large cities, most recently Riga,
Bucharest and Edinburgh.

Sources: the news items of www.sofia.bg (“Новини”, in Bulgarian). The website allows all robots.
The keeper read the 300 news items dated 20 July to 19 October 2026 on 19 October 2026. In them
the keeper looked for sentences that name a future date together with a promise word (86
sentences), then read each candidate in its item. Fourteen sentences from 11 news items became 14
bets; two items give two bets each (sofia-2026-006 and -008) and one item gives three
(sofia-2026-001, -004 and -013). The news items carry no author line; the sender is the City. The
date a statement was said is the date shown for the item in the City's news list.

Left out on purpose: works of other bodies, even when the City reports them (the music rights
society МУЗИКАУТОР, Eurovision 2027 with the national broadcaster BNT and the EBU, the European
tourism capital chosen by an EU jury), and goals without a checkable result (the EU climate
mission); deadlines that had already passed on 19 October 2026; calls for applications, events
and seasons; repeats of the same promise (electric buses “in spring 2027” repeat sofia-2026-013);
spans over several years (roads with the Academy of Sciences 2027–2029); and 93 cultural projects
for 2027 that cannot be counted from outside. The next municipal election is due in autumn 2027;
its exact date was not yet set on 19 October 2026.

Second scan, 27 October 2026: the keeper read the 27 news items added to the list since 16 October
2026 (dated 16 to 27 October) in the same way (14 sentences). Three became bets sofia-2026-015 to
-017. Left out: the airport works around Terminal 2 (by the operator „СОФ Кънект“, not the City),
seasons and events (tree planting every weekend until 29 November, a football tournament), a
target for 2035, and dates that only repeat the term of an EU grant. “Сред заявените намерения”
(among the stated intentions) counts as said with a reservation (`voraussichtlich`, 0.80). For
these three bets there is no Wayback Machine copy yet; the bet says so and names the checksum of
the keeper's local copy.

Reading the dates: a fixed day counts to that day; a month to its last day (“до февруари 2027
г.”, by February 2027, is read in favour of the City as by 28 February); “до януари 2027 г.” (by
January 2027) as by 31 January; “в началото на 2027 г.” (at the beginning of 2027) to the end of
the first quarter, 31 March; “през лятото” (in summer) to 31 August; “до края на 2027 г.” (by the
end of 2027) and “през 2027 г.” (in 2027) to 31 December; “в рамките на следващите шест месеца”
(within the next six months) six months from the day it was said. Each bet says so. Plain
sentences (“трябва да бъде готов”, must be ready; “срокът за изпълнение е”, the deadline is) count
as announced (`angekuendigt`, 1.00). A quoted sentence with “очаква”, “очакваме” (is expected, we
expect), “предвижда”, “предвидени” (foreseen), “планирано” (planned) or “целта е” (the aim is)
counts as said with a reservation (`voraussichtlich`, 0.80). Only the quoted sentence decides.

Language: this edition is in English. Quotations stay in the original Bulgarian, exactly as
published. The question under each bet is the keeper's own wording and is the only thing that is
resolved. English glosses of Bulgarian words in the text are the keeper's; they were made with the
help of a language model and have not yet been checked by a native speaker.

The speaker in this book is Sofia Municipality as an institution. Names of officials are used only
to name a source. A bet is resolved "No" only with a primary source (a news item or page of the
City, a decision of the Sofia Municipal Council, a procurement notice, a permit, a dated photo)
and a date; if in doubt, it is marked `strittig` (disputed). Pages of the City may be updated in
place, so an entry can also be resolved by a dated copy of the page.

Quotations remain (c) Столична община. The terms of use of www.sofia.bg (read 19 October 2026)
say the information on the portal is freely accessible for personal, public and non-commercial
use, and: “Позволено е огласяване на информация от портала в средства за масова информация, без
манипулиране на данни и с изискване да бъде цитиран източникът.” (Publishing information from the
portal in the media is allowed, without manipulating data and on condition that the source is
cited.) This book is not endorsed by Sofia Municipality; the City has not agreed with it or with
any question in it. Each bet quotes one sentence, with the title of the news item, its date and a
link. Quoting also rests on the Bulgarian Copyright Act (Закон за авторското право и сродните му
права, чл. 24, ал. 1, т. 2: quotations for analysis, commentary or criticism, naming the source
and the author) (not checked by a lawyer). Everything else CC0. The source pages are not copied
into this repository. For each source there is a link to a Wayback Machine copy and the SHA-256
checksum of the keeper's local copy. Quotes were checked against the text of the page after
removing markup, decoding entities and collapsing repeated spaces.

The "Computer" in this book is a language model (Claude, Anthropic) with access to public
sources, not special software. It stands in the same list as the City, under the same rules.

Corrections: write to hallo@belegbar.eu (Bulgarian, English and German are fine). Complaints are
checked within 48 hours and noted on the bet concerned; entries are never changed, only
annotated.

The keeper takes no money from the institutions measured here, with one exception: a published
fixed price for entries an institution lodges itself (festgehalten format v1, §8.4). So far:
none.

Expiry: an open bet expires two years after its check date (format §1.1, §2.3). Expired bets do
not count towards the hit rate, but they stay in the record.
