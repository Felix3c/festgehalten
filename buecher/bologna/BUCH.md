Bologna keeps saying what it will do and when: two old-town streets repaved by 13 November, the
final asphalt at a tram junction in November, a new footbridge to the Cassero di Porta Galliera by
the end of January 2027, two new nurseries from January 2027, a new music house at the start of
2027, and air-conditioning in its nurseries and pre-schools by summer 2027. This book writes it
down, waits, and checks. It quotes one speaker: the City of Bologna (Comune di Bologna, with Mayor
Matteo Lepore and the members of his executive, the Giunta), in the press releases on its own
website. A statement goes in if it has a date; intentions without one stay out. Each bet asks only
whether the date in the quote was met, never whether anyone meant it.

Why Bologna: the keeper's books now cover very large cities and capitals only. Bologna, the
seventh-largest city in Italy, comes after books on Rome, Naples, Palermo, Genoa and other
European capitals and large cities.

Sources: the press releases of the City on www.comune.bologna.it (“Comunicati stampa”, in Italian;
the City describes them as “Le comunicazioni ufficiali del Sindaco, degli organi politici e degli
uffici comunali”). Every release names as its author the City's press and institutional
communication office (“Ufficio stampa e comunicazione istituzionale”) in the town hall. The
robots.txt of www.comune.bologna.it (read 21 October 2026) excludes only the paths of the portal
software (`/myportal/`), not the press releases; the other news section of the site loads its text
only from those excluded paths, so the keeper did not use it. On 21 October 2026 the keeper read
the list of releases and all 362 releases dated 21 July to 21 October 2026; the City publishes
about four a day. In them the keeper looked for sentences that name a future date (205 sentences,
plus two further searches), and read them in their release. Fourteen sentences were checked live
on 21 October 2026 and again on 26 October 2026; thirteen became bets. The date a statement was
said is the date on the release's own page (“Pubblicato il”).

Left out on purpose: works and plans of other bodies, even when the City reports them (the region,
the railway, the motorway company, the water and heating utility, the football club, foundations),
and one road closure on the border with San Lazzaro di Savena where the builder is not named;
events, exhibitions and calls with a closing day; orders and programmes that simply run until a
date (such as the regional smog rules); budgets for a period without a promised result; deadlines
that fall before mid-November 2026 or are hard to check from outside (cables laid in November,
equipment handed to the local police by December); and a sentence that left open whether it meant
the start or the end of works. For the tram, in three months of releases the keeper found no date
for passenger service, only for test runs, so there is no bet on it.

Reading the dates: “Termine previsto: 13 novembre” (expected end: 13 November) means by 13
November 2026; “entro novembre” (by November) by 30 November 2026; “a fine gennaio 2027” and “da
gennaio 2027” (at the end of January 2027, from January 2027) by 31 January 2027; “per febbraio
2027” (for February 2027) by 28 February 2027; “a inizio 2027” (at the start of 2027) generously by
31 March 2027, the end of the first quarter; “fino al 26 maggio 2027” (until 26 May 2027) on that
day; “entro il giugno 2027” (by June 2027) by 30 June 2027; “entro l’estate 2027” (by summer 2027)
by 22 September 2027, the last day of summer; “per settembre 2027” (for September 2027) by 30
September 2027; “nel 2028” (in 2028) by 31 December 2028. Each bet says so. Plain sentences
(“si svolgeranno”, “sarà aperta”, “avranno”, will take place, will be open, will have) count as
announced (`angekuendigt`, 1.00). A sentence with “previsto”, “prevista”, “previste” or “salvo
condizioni meteo avverse” (expected, foreseen, unless the weather is bad) counts as said with a
reservation (`voraussichtlich`, 0.80). Where one sentence holds two statements, the bet names the
part it rests on. Only the quoted sentence decides.

The City contradicts itself once, and the book keeps both sides: in August 2026 the councillor for
schools said that all nurseries and pre-schools would have adequate air-conditioning by June 2027
(bologna-2026-010); in September 2026 the City said the pre-schools would be fully air-conditioned
only in 2028 (bologna-2026-013).

Election: the releases name no election date. The next regular municipal election in Bologna is
expected in 2027 (date not checked by the keeper). Bets from May 2027 on (bologna-2026-009 to -013)
may resolve under a new administration; they stay bets on the City, whoever leads it.

Language: this edition is in English. Quotations stay in the original Italian, exactly as
published, including the typographic apostrophe (’) where the City used it. Bets bologna-2026-001
and -002 quote two separate entries of the same weekly works list; nothing is left out in the
middle of a quote. The question under each bet is the
keeper's own wording and is the only thing that is resolved. English glosses of Italian words in
the text are the keeper's; they were made with the help of a language model and have not yet been
checked by a native speaker.

The speaker in this book is the City of Bologna as an institution. Names of officials are used only
to name a source. A bet is resolved "No" only with a primary source (a press release or page of the
City, a decision of the Giunta or the City Council, a notice on the City's notice board, a
procurement notice, a dated photo) and a date; if in doubt, it is marked `strittig` (disputed).
Pages of the City may be updated in place, so an entry can also be resolved by a dated copy of the
page.

Rights: the press office of the City of Bologna (Ufficio stampa) allowed in writing on 26 October
2026 that its press releases be quoted here. Before that, the keeper found no licence statement on
the release pages themselves; the City's legal notes (“Note legali”) are served only through the
excluded portal paths and were not read. Short quotation for criticism and discussion is also
allowed by art. 70 of the Italian copyright law (Legge 633/1941) (not checked by a lawyer). This
book names the source with a link for every quote and uses no pictures, logos or coats of arms. It
is not endorsed by the City of Bologna; the City has not agreed with it or with any question in
it. Everything else CC0. The source pages are not copied into this repository. For each source the
keeper keeps a local copy fetched on 26 October 2026 and gives the SHA-256 checksum of its text
(title and paragraphs), and a link to a Wayback Machine copy saved by the keeper on 21 October
2026; every copy contains its quote word for word, and the text was the same on 21 and 26 October
2026. Quotes were checked against the text after removing markup, decoding entities and collapsing
repeated spaces.

The "Computer" in this book is a language model (Claude, Anthropic) with access to public
sources, not special software. It stands in the same list as the City, under the same rules.

Corrections: write to hallo@belegbar.eu (Italian, English and German are fine). Complaints are
checked within 48 hours and noted on the bet concerned; entries are never changed, only
annotated.

The keeper takes no money from the institutions measured here, with one exception: a published
fixed price for entries an institution lodges itself (festgehalten format v1, §8.4). So far:
none.

Expiry: an open bet expires two years after its check date (format §1.1, §2.3). Expired bets do
not count towards the hit rate, but they stay in the record.
