The Municipality of Budapest (Budapest Főváros Önkormányzata) keeps saying what will be decided,
built and changed, and when: a noise decree, parking zones, an environmental programme, a bridge,
bike sharing, a cut in its own spending. This book writes it down, waits, and checks. It only
quotes what the City itself published in its news (“Hírek”) on budapest.hu. A statement goes in
if it has a date; intentions without one stay out. Each bet asks only whether the date in the
quote was met, never whether anyone meant it.

Why Budapest: the keeper's books now cover very large cities and capitals only. Budapest comes
after London, Paris, Hamburg, Munich, Vienna, Rome, Dublin, Copenhagen, Helsinki and Oslo.

Sources: the news items under https://budapest.hu/hirek/. The keeper listed them through the
City's sitemap: of 1,240 dated news items, 166 were published from 19 April 2026 on. On
18 October 2026 the keeper read those 166 items; 115 sentences named a date in the future,
sixteen were checked word for word, thirteen became bets. The date a statement was said is the
date of the news item in its address. Left out on purpose: a tender for 42 new suburban trains
(HÉV), because the HÉV belongs to the state, not the City; a solar output target of the City's
museum company (BKM), which needs a figure no public source gives; the full rebuilding of the
Grand Boulevard (Nagykörút) by 2029, which stands only in a subordinate clause; deadlines of
grant calls; climate projections; and dates already past. The City's robots.txt closes one
page (a rating form); it was not fetched.

Reading the dates: a month counts to its last day; “ősszel” (in autumn) to 30 November;
“év végéig” or a year alone (“2027-re”, for 2027) to 31 December; a quarter to its last day.
Where the keeper had to set a date (“a jövő év elejére”, by early next year; “2028-tól”, from
2028), the bet says so. A sentence with “várhatóan” (expectedly), “a tervek szerint” (according
to plan) or a verb with “-hat/-het” (can, may) counts as said with a reservation
(`voraussichtlich`, 0.80); a plain statement or a commitment (“vállalta”, undertook) counts as
announced (`angekuendigt`, 1.00).

Language: this edition is in English. Quotations stay in the original Hungarian, exactly as
published. The question under each bet is the keeper's own wording and is the only thing that
is resolved. English glosses of Hungarian words in the text are the keeper's.

Speakers in this book are the Municipality of Budapest as an institution, the Mayor's Office
(Főpolgármesteri Hivatal) and its companies (mostly BKK, the Centre for Budapest Transport, and
Budapest Közút, the road company). Names of officials and Assembly members are not used. The
book measures only the City, not the national government. A bet is resolved "No" only with a
primary source (a news item of the City, a decision or decree of the City Assembly, a notice of
a City company, the City's budget or closing accounts) and a date; if in doubt, it is marked
`strittig` (disputed).

Quotations remain (c) Budapest Főváros Önkormányzata (short quotation for review and discussion
with the source named, Hungarian copyright act, Act LXXVI of 1999, § 34; budapest.hu publishes
no re-use terms the keeper could find; not checked by a lawyer); everything else CC0. The source
pages are not copied into this repository. For each source there is a link to a Wayback Machine
copy, and the SHA-256 checksum of the keeper's local copy. Quotes were checked against the
article text after removing inline markup and collapsing repeated spaces.

The "Computer" in this book is a language model (Claude, Anthropic) with access to public
sources, not special software. It stands in the same list as the City, under the same rules.

Corrections: write to hallo@belegbar.eu (Hungarian, English and German are fine). Complaints
are checked within 48 hours and noted on the bet concerned; entries are never changed, only
annotated.

The keeper takes no money from the institution measured here, with one exception: a
published fixed price for entries an institution lodges itself (festgehalten format v1, §8.4). So far:
none.

Expiry: an open bet expires two years after its check date (format §1.1, §2.3). Expired bets
do not count towards the hit rate, but they stay in the record.
