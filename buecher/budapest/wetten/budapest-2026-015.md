## Context
The news item of 29 October 2026 summarises the 40 proposals for the City Assembly session of Friday 30 October 2026. One of them, based on a decision of the joint council of City and government (Fővárosi Közfejlesztések Tanácsa), asks the Assembly to approve a shareholder and cooperation agreement between the City, the minister for transport and investment, his ministry, the state-owned OKK Országos Közlekedési Központ Zrt. and BKK. Under it, OKK takes a 50 per cent stake in BKK through a capital increase. The item then says, without a reservation, that from 1 January 2027 the company orders City, HÉV, suburban rail and suburban bus services as joint transport organiser under the new name. Said: 2026-10-29; by: Budapest Főváros Önkormányzata.

## Reading
The sentence is a plain statement (“rendeli meg”, orders): `angekuendigt`, 1.00, although it describes a proposal that the Assembly had not yet voted on when it was said. Yes if the company register (cégjegyzék), BKK or the City shows that the company carries the name “BKK Budapesti Várostérségi Közlekedési Központ Zrt.” with effect no later than 1 January 2027 and acts as joint organiser of at least the HÉV or suburban rail or suburban bus services from that day; otherwise No. A name change registered later but backdated to 1 January 2027 counts as Yes. If the Assembly rejects or postpones the proposal, the answer is No. `pruefung_am` is two weeks after the deadline, to give the register time.
