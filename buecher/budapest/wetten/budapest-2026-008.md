## Context
The K-híd (K bridge) links Óbuda with the Hajógyári island, the site of the Sziget festival. Budapest Közút and Strabag were to start its renovation on 12 October 2026; in the first stage, until June 2027, the bridge is closed to traffic while the carriageway and the southern footway are renewed. No work is done during the children's festival in June and Sziget in August. Said: 2026-10-09; by: Budapest Főváros Önkormányzata.
