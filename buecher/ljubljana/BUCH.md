Ljubljana keeps saying what it will do and when: the last Plečnik arcade renovated by the middle
of November 2026, two closed streets open again by mid-December, 100 green bus shelters and no
rise in kindergarten prices in 2026, a bus fleet without fossil fuels by 2030. This book writes it
down, waits, and checks. It quotes one speaker: the City of Ljubljana (Mestna občina Ljubljana,
with Mayor Zoran Janković, his deputy mayors and the City's own housing fund), in the news items on
its own website. A statement goes in if it has a date; intentions without one stay out. Each bet
asks only whether the date in the quote was met, never whether anyone meant it.

The municipal election in Slovenia is on Sunday 15 November 2026 (“Lokalne volitve 2026 bodo v
nedeljo, 15. novembra 2026.”, City website, read 19 October 2026). Every check date in this book
falls after it; the first is the day after (ljubljana-2026-001). Two bets quote the mayor himself
before the election (ljubljana-2026-005 and -006). The speaker in this book stays the City as an
institution, whoever governs it after the election.

Why Ljubljana: the keeper's books now cover very large cities and capitals only. Ljubljana, the
capital of Slovenia, comes after books on other European capitals and large cities, most recently
Bucharest, Edinburgh and Sofia.

Sources: the news items of www.ljubljana.si (“Novice”, in Slovenian). The website's robots.txt
allows all robots except for its admin area. The City publishes about one and a half news items a
day, so the keeper read the 300 most recent, dated 10 April to 19 October 2026, on 19 October 2026.
In them the keeper looked for sentences that name a future date together with a promise word (62
sentences, plus a second pass by hand), then read each candidate in its item. Fourteen sentences
were checked live and in the archive; eleven became bets, one per news item. The news items carry
no author line; the sender is the City. The date a statement was said is the date on the item's
own page.

Left out on purpose: works of other bodies, even when the City reports them (the regional e-bike
system, regional cycle routes with national funds, the district heating plant of Energetika
Ljubljana, the tourism board); deadlines that had already passed on 19 October 2026; calls for
applications, events and exhibitions with a closing day; rules rather than promises (free parking
permits for residents until 31 March 2027); the tree and avenue of the year, to be announced “in
spring”; tree felling on Golovec “expectedly in December”, which cannot be checked from outside;
and strategy goals without a checkable number.

Reading the dates: a fixed day counts to that day; “do (predvidoma) 14. decembra” (until,
expectedly, 14 December) means open again by that day; “sredina novembra” (the middle of
November) is read in favour of the City as 15 November; “do konca letošnjega leta”, “še letos”
and “letos” (by the end of this year, still this year, this year) as 31 December 2026; “v
prihodnjem letu” (next year) as the calendar year 2027; “v letih 2027 in 2028” (in 2027 and 2028)
as by 31 December 2028; “do leta 2030” and “do leta 2035” (by the year …) in favour of the City as
by 31 December of that year; “najmanj do junija 2027” (at least until June 2027) as still in use
when June 2027 begins. Each bet says so. Plain sentences (“bomo”, we will; “ne bo”, there will not
be) count as announced (`angekuendigt`, 1.00). A quoted sentence with “predvidoma” (expectedly) or
“načrtujemo” (we plan) counts as said with a reservation (`voraussichtlich`, 0.80). Only the
quoted sentence decides.

Language: this edition is in English. Quotations stay in the original Slovenian, exactly as
published. The question under each bet is the keeper's own wording and is the only thing that is
resolved. English glosses of Slovenian words in the text are the keeper's; they were made with the
help of a language model and have not yet been checked by a native speaker.

The speaker in this book is the City of Ljubljana as an institution. Names of officials are used
only to name a source. A bet is resolved "No" only with a primary source (a news item or page of
the City, a decision of the City Council, a notice in the Official Gazette, a procurement notice,
a permit, a dated photo) and a date; if in doubt, it is marked `strittig` (disputed). Pages of the
City may be updated in place, so an entry can also be resolved by a dated copy of the page.

Quotations remain (c) Mestna občina Ljubljana. The keeper found no terms of use for
www.ljubljana.si on 19 October 2026: the footer names only the copyright of the City, and the
privacy rules refer to general terms of use that are not linked. This book is not endorsed by
Mestna občina Ljubljana; the City has not agreed with it or with any question in it. Each bet quotes
one sentence, with the title of the news item, its date and a link, and no pictures. Quoting rests
on the Slovenian Copyright and Related Rights Act (Zakon o avtorski in sorodnih pravicah, ZASP,
Article 51 “Citati”, version in force since 26 October 2022: quoting excerpts of a published work
is free where needed for illustration, comparison or reference, naming the source and the author
where the work names one) (not checked by a lawyer). Everything else CC0. The source pages are not
copied into this repository. For each source there is a link to a Wayback Machine copy and the
SHA-256 checksum of the keeper's local copy. Quotes were checked against the text of the page after
removing markup, decoding entities and collapsing repeated spaces.

The "Computer" in this book is a language model (Claude, Anthropic) with access to public
sources, not special software. It stands in the same list as the City, under the same rules.

Corrections: write to hallo@belegbar.eu (Slovenian, English and German are fine). Complaints are
checked within 48 hours and noted on the bet concerned; entries are never changed, only
annotated.

The keeper takes no money from the institutions measured here, with one exception: a published
fixed price for entries an institution lodges itself (festgehalten format v1, §8.4). So far:
none.

Expiry: an open bet expires two years after its check date (format §1.1, §2.3). Expired bets do
not count towards the hit rate, but they stay in the record.
