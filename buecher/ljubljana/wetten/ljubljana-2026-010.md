## Context
From the September 2026 issue of the City's newsletter “Ljubljana”, on 125 years of public transport in Ljubljana and the City's vision for 2045 (Vizija Supermesto 2045). LPP, the bus company, belongs to the City's public holding. On 12 November 2025 the first hydrogen bus ran in Ljubljana. Said: 2026-09-03; by: Mestna občina Ljubljana.

## Reading
“Načrtujemo” (we plan) is a reservation: `voraussichtlich`, 0.80. “Do leta 2030” (by the year 2030) is read in favour of the City as by 31 December 2030. Yes if a City or LPP source dated by 31 March 2031 says that every bus in the LPP city fleet runs on a carbon-neutral fuel (for example electricity, hydrogen or biomethane) and no bus runs on diesel or fossil natural gas; if the City counts a fuel as carbon-neutral that its own sources do not, `strittig`. Otherwise No.
