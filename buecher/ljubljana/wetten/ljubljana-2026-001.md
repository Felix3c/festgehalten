## Context
The City is renovating the last arcade and loggia of Plečnik's market on Adamič-Lundrovo nabrežje that had not yet been restored: roof, façade, windows and doors, and the asphalt under the arcades, under the conditions of the Slovenian heritage institute (Zavod za varstvo kulturne dediščine Slovenije). Works began on Monday 3 August 2026. The municipal election is on the same Sunday, 15 November 2026. Said: 2026-07-30; by: Mestna občina Ljubljana.
