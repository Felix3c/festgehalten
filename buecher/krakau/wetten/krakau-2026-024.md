## Context
Under the rules now in force, the monthly pass for Kraków's Clean Transport Zone (Strefa Czystego Transportu, SCT) rises to 250 zł in 2027 and 500 zł in 2028. The City wants to keep the 2026 fees (100 zł a month) for both years. Residents can comment from 6 to 27 November. The City says the higher fees would apply from 1 January 2027 unless the Council acts, so it split the change into two stages and the freeze comes first. Said: 2026-10-30; by: Gmina Miejska Kraków.
