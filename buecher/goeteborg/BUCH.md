Gothenburg keeps saying what it will do and when: a new outdoor gym in Lundby on 3 November, a
new library at Friskväderstorget on 7 November, Sankt Eriksgatan open on 13 December, the new
bridge in Gamlestaden at the turn of the year, the City's art gallery in the old slaughterhouse on
20 March 2027, parks, tunnels, a skate plaza and a football arena during 2027. This book writes it
down, waits, and checks. It quotes one speaker: the City of Gothenburg (Göteborgs Stad, with its
committees and administrations), in the news items it publishes on its own website. A statement
goes in if it has a date; intentions without one stay out. Each bet asks only whether the date in
the quote was met, never whether anyone meant it.

Why Gothenburg: the keeper's books now cover very large cities and capitals only. Gothenburg, the
second-largest city in Sweden, comes after books on Oslo, Copenhagen, Helsinki and other
European capitals and large cities.

Sources: Göteborgs Stad (goteborg.se, Aktuelltarkiv), the City's archive of news items
(“Aktuelltarkiv”, in Swedish), written by the City and its administrations (among them the land
development, urban environment, and sports and associations administrations) and by its committees
(short reports from their meetings). The website's robots.txt (read 23 October 2026) excludes, among
other things, portal state addresses of the archive (“/wps/portal/aktuelltarkivet/!ut/”); this book
used none of them, only the plain list pages and the plain item pages. The City's press room on
Mynewsdesk was not used: older pages there are reached only through an address pattern that
Mynewsdesk's robots.txt excludes. On 23 October 2026 the keeper read 360 items; 350 of them are
dated 20 April to 23 October 2026. Most are traffic notices, events, deadlines and committee news.
In them the keeper looked for sentences that name a future date together with a promise word (186
sentences in 98 items), then read each candidate in its item. Twenty-four sentences were checked
live and in the archive; all twenty-four became bets. The date a statement was said is the date
“Publicerad” (published) on the item's own page.

Left out on purpose: works of others, even when the City reports them (the Västlänken rail tunnel
and its stations, built by the national transport administration Trafikverket; housing by private
or public companies; the City's energy company Göteborg Energi; projects with EU or Vinnova
funding periods); events, exhibitions and deadlines with a closing day; fees from 1 January 2027
(a tariff, not a building); plans without a result that can be seen (starts of detailed
development plans, decisions planned for 2027, a museum card that the City Council has only been
asked to approve, school changes from the school years 2027/28 and 2028/29 that the City makes
subject to conditions and a court); goals without a checkable end (climate neutral by 2030) or
without saying which places (“several playgrounds” this autumn); dates too far away for a book that
starts now (2028 and later); and an internal change that could hardly be checked from outside
(digital signing of care work in October 2027). Where the City gave a date in one item and a
different date in a later one, the book bets on the later date and says so: the bridge in
Gamlestaden (first autumn 2027, now the turn of the year), the changing-room building and the
machine hall at Heden (first the beginning and spring of 2027, now summer 2027) and the skate plaza
at Slottsskogsvallen (first summer, now autumn 2027). Where two items repeated the same promise
with the same date, only one was used. No promise had already been kept before this book began.

Reading the dates: a fixed day counts to that day; a month to its last day (“i november”, “i
december”, “februari 2027”); “mellan oktober och november 2026” (between October and November
2026) to 30 November 2026; “i början av december” (at the beginning of December) to 10 December
2026; “fram till jul” (until Christmas) to 24 December 2026; “vid årsskiftet 2026/2027” (at the
turn of the year 2026/2027) to 31 January 2027, one month after New Year, a reading the keeper set;
“januari–mars 2027” to 31 March 2027; “våren” (spring) to 31 May; “till sommaren” (by summer) to
30 June; “sommaren” (summer) to 31 August; “hösten” (autumn) to 30 November; a year alone (“2027”,
“under 2027”) to 31 December. Each bet says so. Plain sentences (“invigs”, “öppnar”, “börjar”, “ska
öppna”, “ska vara klart”; is inaugurated, opens, begins, shall open, shall be finished) and
committee decisions with a date count as announced (`angekuendigt`, 1.00). A sentence with
“beräknas”, “planeras”, “väntas”, “planen är” or “preliminär” (is expected, is planned, is
foreseen, the plan is, preliminary) counts as said with a reservation (`voraussichtlich`, 0.80).
Where one sentence holds two statements, the bet names the part it rests on. Only the quoted
sentence decides.

Language: this edition is in English. Quotations stay in the original Swedish, exactly as
published. The question under each bet is the keeper's own wording and is the only thing that is
resolved. English glosses of Swedish words in the text are the keeper's; they were made with the
help of a language model and have not yet been checked by a native speaker.

The speaker in this book is the City of Gothenburg as an institution. Names of officials are used
only to name a source. A bet is resolved "No" only with a primary source (a news item or page of
the City, a decision or minutes of a City committee or the City Council, a procurement notice, a
dated photo) and a date; if in doubt, it is marked `strittig` (disputed). Pages of the City may be
updated in place, so an entry can also be resolved by a dated copy of the page.

Rights: unclear. Quotations remain (c) Göteborgs Stad. The keeper found no terms of use and no
licence for the texts of goteborg.se: the page “Om webbplatsen” (about the website, read 23 October
2026) deals only with cookies, logging in and searching, and the footer says only “goteborg.se är
Göteborgs Stads officiella webbplats.” This book therefore takes from each item one sentence, with
the title of the item, its date and a link, for criticism and review; that is what the quotation
right in Swedish copyright law allows (upphovsrättslagen, 22 §). Whether the items are free of
copyright as statements of Swedish authorities (upphovsrättslagen, 9 §) is also unclear. Neither
point has been checked by a lawyer. This book uses no pictures or coats of arms; the City's photos
name their photographers and are never copied. This book is not endorsed by the City of
Gothenburg; the City does not support it and has not agreed with it or with any question in it.
Everything else CC0. The source pages are not copied into this repository. For each source there is
a link to a Wayback Machine copy and the SHA-256 checksum of the keeper's local copy. Quotes were
checked against the text of the page after removing inline markup, decoding entities and collapsing
repeated spaces. The City's pages carry style and script addresses that change on every visit, so
a later copy of a page is never byte-for-byte the same; the quote is what was checked.

The "Computer" in this book is a language model (Claude, Anthropic) with access to public
sources, not special software. It stands in the same list as the City, under the same rules.

Corrections: write to hallo@belegbar.eu (Swedish, English and German are fine). Complaints are
checked within 48 hours and noted on the bet concerned; entries are never changed, only
annotated.

The keeper takes no money from the institutions measured here, with one exception: a published
fixed price for entries an institution lodges itself (festgehalten format v1, §8.4). So far:
none.

Expiry: an open bet expires two years after its check date (format §1.1, §2.3). Expired bets do
not count towards the hit rate, but they stay in the record.
