## Context
For the anniversary of the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child, the City holds its yearly children's rights day with UNICEF at the Hôtel de Ville on 18 November 2026 (theme “Grandir en sécurité”). Lyon has been part of UNICEF's “Ville amie des enfants” (child-friendly city) programme since 2004. The news item announces that the partnership is to be renewed for the 2026–2032 term.
