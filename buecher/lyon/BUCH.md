Lyon keeps saying what it will do and when: a Christmas market, a citizens' convention, free
school supplies, a bath in the river dock at Confluence, more municipal police. This book writes
it down, waits, and checks. It quotes one speaker: the City of Lyon (Ville de Lyon, the
municipality, not the Métropole de Lyon), on its own website. A statement goes in if it has a
date; intentions without one stay out. Each bet asks only whether the date in the quote was
met, never whether anyone meant it.

Why Lyon: the keeper's books now cover very large cities and capitals only. Lyon, the third
largest city of France, comes after London, Paris, Hamburg, Munich, Vienna, Rome, Dublin,
Copenhagen, Helsinki, Oslo and Brussels.

Sources: the news list of www.lyon.fr (“Toute l'actualité”, pages 0 to 25) held 233 news items
from December 2025 to 15 October 2026; all 233 were read on 19 October 2026. In them the keeper
looked for sentences that name a future date together with a promise word (38 candidates), then
read each candidate in its item. Thirteen became bets. The date a statement was said is the date
printed on the news item.

Left out on purpose: deadlines of calls and applications; events, exhibitions and seasons;
works of other bodies that the City only reports (lifts and escalators of the metro and the bus
station Perrache, run by TCL/Sytral Mobilités; the 2030 Winter Olympics, run by their organising
committee); a second sentence on free school supplies that repeats lyon-2026-007; duties that
fall on associations rather than the City (projects of traders' associations, food-truck
permits); an excavation “jusqu'en 2028” (until 2028) where it is unclear what “done” means.

Reading the dates: a fixed day counts to that day; a month to its last day; “été” (summer) to
31 August; a year alone (“en 2027”) to 31 December. “À la rentrée 2027-2028” (at the start of
the 2027–2028 school year) is set by the keeper to 30 September 2027, “à partir de 2028” (from
2028) to 31 December 2028, and “fin du mandat” (end of the term) to 31 March 2032, the month of
the next ordinary municipal elections; each bet says so. Where a year names works without saying
whether they start or end, the keeper reads the start and the bet says so. A quoted sentence with
“devrait” (should), “prévu” (planned), “objectif” (goal or target) or “espère” (hopes) counts as
said with a reservation (`voraussichtlich`, 0.80); a plain statement, heading or schedule line
counts as announced (`angekuendigt`, 1.00). Only the quoted sentence decides.

Language: this edition is in English. Quotations stay in the original French, exactly as
published. The question under each bet is the keeper's own wording and is the only thing that
is resolved. English glosses of French words in the text are the keeper's.

The speaker in this book is the Ville de Lyon as an institution. Names of officials are used
only to name a source. A bet is resolved "No" only with a primary source (a page or news item of
the City, a deliberation of the City Council, a permit, a dated photo) and a date; if in doubt,
it is marked `strittig` (disputed). Pages of the City are updated in place, so an entry can also
be resolved by a dated copy of the page.

Quotations remain (c) Ville de Lyon (short quotation, art. L122-5 3° a) Code de la propriété
intellectuelle; the legal notice of lyon.fr names no open licence; not checked by a lawyer);
everything else CC0. The source pages are not copied into this repository. For each source there
is a link to a Wayback Machine copy, and the SHA-256 checksum of the keeper's local copy. Quotes
were checked against the text after removing inline markup and collapsing repeated spaces.

The "Computer" in this book is a language model (Claude, Anthropic) with access to public
sources, not special software. It stands in the same list as the City, under the same rules.

Corrections: write to hallo@belegbar.eu (French, English and German are fine). Complaints are
checked within 48 hours and noted on the bet concerned; entries are never changed, only
annotated.

The keeper takes no money from the institutions measured here, with one exception: a published
fixed price for entries an institution lodges itself (festgehalten format v1, §8.4). So far:
none.

Expiry: an open bet expires two years after its check date (format §1.1, §2.3). Expired bets do
not count towards the hit rate, but they stay in the record.
