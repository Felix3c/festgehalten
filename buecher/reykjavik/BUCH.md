Reykjavík keeps saying what it will do and when: six more school buildings in Laugardalur this
autumn, a new structure for its welfare and school services by 1 January 2027, a tender for waste
collection in Breiðholt, and a list of 190 tasks of the new City Council majority, each with its
own deadline. This book writes it down, waits, and checks. It quotes two speakers. The City of
Reykjavík (Reykjavíkurborg) speaks in the news items on its website. The new majority in the City
Council, formed by the Independence Party, the Liberal Reform Party and the Progressive Party
(mayor Hildur Björnsdóttir), speaks in its own task list on verkefnalisti.is. Each bet names its
speaker. A statement goes in if it has a date; intentions without one stay out. Each bet asks only
whether the date in the quote was met, never whether anyone meant it.

Why Reykjavík: the keeper's books now cover very large cities and capitals only. Reykjavík, the
capital of Iceland, comes after books on other European capitals, most recently Luxembourg,
Bratislava and Zagreb.

Sources. (1) The news items of reykjavik.is: the 252 Icelandic items since 8 April 2026 (/frettir) and
the 39 items of the English news list (/en/news, from 13 May 2026), read on 19 October 2026; about
twelve sentences in them name a future date. The City publishes most items in Icelandic and English; this book quotes
the City's English version and has checked that the Icelandic original says the same (each bet
names the Icelandic sentence). (2) The task list (“verkefnalisti”) of the new majority. On 27 May
2026 the City reported the presentation of the majority at Viðey and said that the coalition
agreement would be published in full on verkefnalisti.is, with dated tasks residents can follow,
going online on 2 June 2026, when the majority formally took office at the City Council meeting.
The page www.verkefnalisti.is/en loads its items from an open address
(https://verkefnalisti-xd.vercel.app/api/tasks?collection=en), which this book quotes word for
word. The list has 190 tasks in three groups: “100 DAYS” (31), “12 MONTHS” (73) and “4 YEARS” (86).
It does not say from which day the groups count. The keeper counts from 2 June 2026, the day the
majority took office and the list went online: 100 days end on Thursday 10 September 2026,
12 months on Wednesday 2 June 2027. The robots.txt of reykjavik.is (Drupal defaults only) and of
www.verkefnalisti.is (open, with “Content-Signal: search=yes, ai-input=yes, ai-train=yes”) were
read on 19 October 2026; nothing used here is excluded.

What went in: 23 bets. Fourteen are tasks of the “12 MONTHS” group whose result leaves a public
trace (admission rules, a published table, a tender, a council decision, an opening, published
opening hours, tax rates). Nine are dated sentences in City news items; one of them, the swap of
two offices on 1 June 2027, is a statement of the majority reported by the City and is counted for
the majority. Left out on purpose: 55 other tasks of the 12-month group, because their result
cannot be told apart from ongoing work (talks with the state, “strengthen”, “map”, “review”); two
tasks with a 30-day processing time, because the keeper does not know whether the City publishes
such figures; the task on commercial property tax alone, which is part of
reykjavik-2026-018; the task on tendering waste collection, which reykjavik-2026-007 checks with
the City's own date; the 100-day tasks, whose deadline had passed (see below); the 4-year tasks,
because the term has no fixed last day; the Borgarlína works at Nauthólsvegur and the Fossvogur
bridge (due July and summer 2028), which are built with Betri samgöngur and the state, not by the
City alone; and dated sentences about yearly events, a cycling plan without an end, a state road
tunnel “in the years 2027–2030”, and school changes for autumn 2026 that had already happened.

Dates said: news items carry their own date, and that date is used. The task list shows no date
for its items, and the keeper found no archive copy of the items older than 19 October 2026; for
the 14 tasks the bet uses the day the keeper read the list (19 October 2026), although the City
said the list would go online on 2 June 2026. A Wayback Machine copy of the start page of
www.verkefnalisti.is of 2 June 2026 (13:11 UTC) shows the site online, but the items are loaded
separately and are not in that copy.

Reading the dates: “this fall” (Icelandic “í haust”) is read as meteorological autumn, so to 30
November; “November” to 30 November; “December” to 31 December; “by Jan. 1, 2027” to 1 January
2027; “early 2027” (“í ársbyrjun 2027”, “í byrjun 2027”) to 31 March 2027; “fall 2027” to 30
November 2027; the group “12 MONTHS” of the task list to 2 June 2027. Each bet says so. A plain
sentence (“will”; Icelandic “mun”, “munu”, “verður”) and a plain line of the task list (“We will”;
Icelandic “Við ætlum að”) count as announced (`angekuendigt`, 1.00). A sentence with “is
expected”, “is scheduled”, “officials expect” or “the goal is” (Icelandic “gert er ráð fyrir”,
“stefnt er að”, “stefnt er á”, “áætlað”) counts as said with a reservation (`voraussichtlich`,
0.80). Where one sentence holds several statements, the bet names the part it rests on. Only the
quoted sentence decides; where the English and the Icelandic versions differ, the bet says so and
rests on what both say.

Language: this edition is in English. The quotations are the City's and the majority's own English
versions, exactly as published; each has an Icelandic original, which the keeper read as well. The
question under each bet is the keeper's own wording and is the only thing that is resolved. English
glosses of Icelandic words in the text are the keeper's; they were made with the help of a
language model and have not been checked by a native speaker.

Observation, not a bet: on 19 October 2026 the Icelandic task list marked all 31 tasks of the
100-day group as “Lokið” (completed), while the English list marked the same 31 tasks as 6
“Completed”, 3 “Started” and 22 without a status. In the 12-month group the Icelandic list showed
2 “Lokið”, 4 “Hafið” (started) and 2 “Í vinnslu” (in progress), the English list no status at all.
Either the English list is not kept up to date or the Icelandic one reports more than is shown;
which, the keeper has not checked.

The speakers in this book are the City of Reykjavík and the City Council majority as
institutions. Names of officials are used only to name a source. A bet is resolved "No" only with
a primary source (a news item or page of the City, minutes of the City Council or its councils, a
procurement notice, the planning portal Skipulagsgátt, a dated photo) and a date; if in doubt, it
is marked `strittig` (disputed). Pages of the City and the task list are updated in place, so an
entry can also be resolved by a dated copy of a page.

Rights: the keeper found no terms of use and no licence for the texts of reykjavik.is (the footer,
the privacy policy and the 32,785 addresses of the sitemap were searched for terms and copyright
pages on 19 October 2026; only terms for swimming cards, summer jobs and payments came up), and
the pages carry no copyright notice. The footer of verkefnalisti.is is an unfilled template
(“© 2026 All Rights Reserved | Company Name”); no terms of use were found there either. The
Icelandic Copyright Act (Höfundalög nr. 73/1972) allows quoting published works “in connection
with criticism, science, general information or another recognised purpose”, within reasonable
limits and correctly rendered (Article 14, keeper's translation); the same chapter asks that the
source be named. The keeper read it
on 19 October 2026 in a Wayback Machine copy of althingi.is of 7 October 2026 (the live page asked
for a browser check and was not opened). This book takes from each item one sentence or one task
line, with the title of the item, its date and a link, to check it; it has not been checked by a
lawyer. This book uses no pictures or coats of arms. It is not endorsed by the City of Reykjavík
or by the parties of the majority; they have not agreed with it or with any question in it.
Everything else CC0. The source pages are not copied into this repository. For each source there
is a link to a Wayback Machine copy and the SHA-256 checksum of the keeper's local copy. Quotes
were checked against the text of the page after removing markup, decoding entities and collapsing
repeated spaces; tasks were checked against the exact title in the task list.

The "Computer" in this book is a language model (Claude, Anthropic) with access to public
sources, not special software. It stands in the same list as the City and the majority, under the
same rules.

Corrections: write to hallo@belegbar.eu (Icelandic, English and German are fine). Complaints are
checked within 48 hours and noted on the bet concerned; entries are never changed, only
annotated.

The keeper takes no money from the institutions measured here, with one exception: a published
fixed price for entries an institution lodges itself (festgehalten format v1, §8.4). So far:
none.

Expiry: an open bet expires two years after its check date (format §1.1, §2.3). Expired bets do
not count towards the hit rate, but they stay in the record.
