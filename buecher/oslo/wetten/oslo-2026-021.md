## Context
This is the second half of the timetable on the Røa tunnel page; oslo-2026-018 asks only about the notice of start. The page is not consistent: this sentence says the hearing runs from mid-November 2026, while the list “Viktige datoer” and the section on hearings say “årsskiftet 2026/2027” (the turn of the year). The date said is the last change of the page in the City's sitemap. Said: 2026-10-23; by: Oslo kommune.

## Reading
“etter planen” (according to plan) is a reservation: `voraussichtlich`, 0.80. Yes if Oslo kommune (project page, Medvirkningsportalen, Plan- og bygningsetaten or a public notice) shows the hearing of the planning programme open on or before Monday 30 November 2026; the two weeks after “midten av november” are a margin in the City's favour. Otherwise No, also if the City follows the “turn of the year” wording. `pruefung_am` is the day after.

## Computer's reasoning
The open office on 24–25 November is announced for the start of planning and the hearing of the programme, which speaks for November. Against that, the same page twice says the turn of the year, and the programme was still being written this autumn. Planning hearings slip more often than notices of start, because the documents must be finished first. About 0.40. Entered on 2026-10-31, before `pruefung_am`.
