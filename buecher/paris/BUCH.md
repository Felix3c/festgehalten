The City of Paris keeps saying what will happen and when: openings, works, prices, school
timetables. This book writes it down, waits, and checks. It only quotes what the Ville de
Paris itself said in its own press releases and press kits (presse.paris.fr). A statement
goes in if it has a date or a number; intentions without either stay out. Each bet asks
only whether the date or number in the quote was met, never whether anyone meant it.

Why Paris: the keeper's books now cover very large cities and capitals only. Paris is the
second after London. The City publishes about eight to nine press releases a month; many
dates sit in the PDF press kits attached to them, not in the release itself.

Sources: the press release list at https://presse.paris.fr/communiques (six per page). On
8 October 2026 the keeper read the titles of all 53 releases since the Mayor took office
(election in the Conseil de Paris on 29 March 2026), and the full text of 13 releases and 2
press kits; on 16 October the three releases published since; on 28 October the five published since
(16 to 22 October: the riots of 17 October, the curfew, the 24 October demonstrations, a
speech), none of which has a dated or numbered promise of the City; on 30 October the one
published since (29 October: 3 million euros for the Region's emergency fund for shopkeepers,
2,500 euros per damaged shop), which has amounts but no date, so no bet. Seven bets come from five of
these documents. Left out on purpose: calls to action without a promise ("mobilisons-nous"),
promises whose outcome cannot be seen from outside ("planning completed"), dates already
past, and promises made by the State or the Préfecture rather than the City.

Language: this edition is in English. Quotations stay in the original French, exactly as
published, including the typographic apostrophe (’). The question under each bet is the
keeper's own wording and is the only thing that is resolved.

Speakers in this book are the Ville de Paris as an institution or the Mayor of Paris in
office. Names of officials are not used. A bet is resolved "No" only with a primary source
(release of the City, deliberation of the Conseil de Paris, published tariff) and a date;
if in doubt, it is marked `strittig` (disputed).

Quotations remain (c) Ville de Paris (short quotation, art. L122-5 3° a) Code de la
propriété intellectuelle); everything else CC0. The source pages are not copied into this
repository. For each source there is a link to a Wayback Machine copy, and the SHA-256
checksum of the keeper's local copy. Quotes were checked against the text after collapsing
repeated spaces.

The "Computer" in this book is a language model (Claude, Anthropic) with access to public
sources, not special software. It stands in the same list as the City, under the same rules.

Corrections: write to hallo@belegbar.eu (French and English are fine). Complaints are
checked within 48 hours and noted on the bet concerned; entries are never changed, only
annotated.

The keeper takes no money from the institution measured here, with one exception: a
published fixed price for entries an institution lodges itself (festgehalten format v1, §8.4). So far:
none.

Expiry: an open bet expires two years after its check date (format §1.1, §2.3). Expired bets
do not count towards the hit rate, but they stay in the record.
